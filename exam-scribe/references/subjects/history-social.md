# Subject profile: history and social sciences

- **Priorities:** key terms and concepts, dates and periods, actors, causes and consequences, and the
  arguments and interpretations the book presents (including whose interpretation it is).
- **Timelines:** `timeline` blocks with `event: <date> | <what happened> [p.N: "..."]`. Dates must be copied
  exactly; the number check enforces it.
- **Causes and effects:** `chain` blocks (`link: cause -> effect [p.N: "..."]`). Do not add causes the book does not state.
- **Interpretations:** attribute them ("According to X, ..."; "The author argues ..."). A claim stated as fact
  when the book presents it as one view will be marked PARTIAL.
- **Essays** (exam format `essay`): the chapter gets an `outline` block: a likely prompt, a thesis and cited
  supporting points, plus a counterpoint.
- **Questions:** mcq and cloze on terms and dates, ordering questions, short "explain why" and "compare"
  questions, evaluate-level questions on competing interpretations.
