# Subject profile: general

Use when no other profile fits.

- **Priorities:** definitions of key terms, the main claims of each section and the evidence the book gives,
  and the book's own examples.
- **Blocks:** `concept` for every key term; `process` for any sequence of steps; `compare` for pairs the book
  contrasts; `chain` for cause-and-effect arguments.
- **Questions:** mcq and cloze on terms; short "why" questions on the main arguments; true/false on the
  distinctions the book draws.
- **Watch for:** hedged claims ("often", "in most cases", "some researchers"): keep the hedge. Dropping it turns
  a true statement into a false one and a careful checker will mark it PARTIAL.
