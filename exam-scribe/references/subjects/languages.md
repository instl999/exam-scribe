# Subject profile: languages

- **Priorities:** vocabulary with meanings and examples, grammar rules with their exceptions, fixed
  expressions, and the book's example sentences.
- **Vocabulary:** one `concept` per key word or expression the book highlights; `example:` with the book's
  sentence. Keep spelling, accents and gender markers exactly as printed.
- **Grammar:** a `rule` block per rule: `rule:` (as the book states it), `element:` for each form or step,
  `exception:` for irregular cases, `case:` for example sentences.
- **Notes language:** when the notes are in the student's language, `term-original:` keeps the target-language
  form exactly; quotes stay in the book's language.
- **Questions:** cloze on forms (conjugations, articles), mcq on meaning and usage, true/false on rules and
  exceptions, short translation questions only when the book provides the answer.
