# Subject profile: economics and business

- **Priorities:** definitions, models and their assumptions ("ceteris paribus", "perfect competition"),
  formulas (elasticity, NPV, ratios), graphs (what shifts which curve, and why), and worked examples.
- **Assumptions are conditions:** put a model's assumptions in `holds-when:` or in the concept's `why:`;
  a result stated without its assumptions is only partly true.
- **Graphs:** use `figure` blocks for the book's diagrams: `what:` (the axes and curves) and `look-for:`
  (the shift and its effect), each cited.
- **Calculations:** `calc:` lines for elasticities, present values and ratios (percent values as `12[%]`).
- **Comparisons:** short run/long run, nominal/real, fixed/variable cost, microeconomics/macroeconomics.
- **Questions:** numeric problems, "what happens to price and quantity if ..." mcqs, true/false on assumptions.
