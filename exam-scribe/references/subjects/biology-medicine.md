# Subject profile: biology and medicine

- **Priorities:** terminology, structures and their functions, processes and pathways (in order),
  classifications, and the book's figures (labelled diagrams are often examined directly).
- **Processes:** use a `process` block with one `step:` per stage, each cited. The flashcards then ask
  "what comes after ...?" for every step. Keep the book's order and names exactly.
- **Figures:** fill `what:` and `look-for:` for every labelled diagram; the cropped image appears in the notes.
- **Comparisons:** mitosis/meiosis, DNA/RNA, prokaryote/eukaryote, arteries/veins, innate/adaptive immunity:
  use `compare` rows for each distinguishing feature.
- **Medicine:** keep qualifiers such as "first-line", "in adults", "contraindicated in", "most common".
  Doses, thresholds and values must be copied exactly (the number check enforces this).
- **Mnemonics** are useful here (`ai-mnemonic`, labeled as AI-added), but only for lists the book gives.
- **Questions:** mcq on structure/function, cloze on terms, ordering and "which step comes next" questions,
  short "explain why" questions for mechanisms.
