# Subject profile: chemistry

- **Priorities:** definitions, equations with conditions (constant pressure, ideal gas, no phase change),
  sign conventions (ΔH < 0 is exothermic; q > 0 means absorbed), units, stoichiometry and worked examples.
- **Formulas:** keep the book's symbols and sign convention exactly; conventions differ between textbooks
  (for example the sign of work) and the exam follows this book. Put conditions in `holds-when:`.
- **Calculations:** use `calc:` with units: `250[g] * 4.184[J/(g*degC)] * 15.0[degC]`, molar masses in `[g/mol]`,
  concentrations in `[M]`, pressures in `[atm]` or `[kPa]`. Temperature differences can use `degC`; gas-law
  temperatures must be kelvin: `(25.0 + 273.15)[K]`.
- **Reactions:** write formulas as the book does; subscripts extracted as `N_2` can be written N₂ in your
  own sentences, but quotes must match the book text.
- **Comparisons:** endothermic/exothermic, heat/temperature, system/surroundings, intensive/extensive,
  acid/base, oxidation/reduction are classic traps; fill the `compare` blocks the skeleton proposes.
- **Questions:** numeric problems, sign questions (true/false), classification mcqs, cloze on definitions.
